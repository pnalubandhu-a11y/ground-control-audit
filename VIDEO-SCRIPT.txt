--- BEAT 4 : NCCL ON TCP - THE HEADLINE  (1:10 - 1:42)   <-- slow down
    audit -u nccl_slow --fixture fixtures/nccl_tcp_fallback --------------
WHAT : runs nccl_slow against real NCCL logs where NCCL fell back to TCP.
SAY  : "Now the big one. GPUs sync data during training through a library
        called NCCL. Normally it uses RDMA - the fast path where network cards

        to ordinary TCP - about a hundred times slower - looking just like 'the
        cluster is slow.' The verdict names the cause AND the exact fix."
DO   : press Enter.
NOTES: the money moment. The insidious part: NOTHING is broken - every hardware
       check (B12/B13/B14) is green, the fabric is healthy. It's a CONFIG
       regression (wrong NCCL_IB_HCA) that looks like generic slowness, so
       teams burn days. The verdict names it + the knob. If asked: this exact
       class hit a real GB300 customer this month and took a multi-day
       war-room; Ground Control turns that into a one-line verdict. Business case.

--- BEAT 5 : A DEAD RDMA PORT  (1:42 - 1:58)

WHAT : runs preflight on a fabric-fault capture (down port, errors, congestion).
SAY  : "A dead network port. One port down, another throwing errors, a third

DO   : press Enter.
NOTES: three network symptoms at once - B13 port DOWN (mlx5_5), B14 rising

       a node's parallel NIC-to-switch paths - lose one and multi-node
       bandwidth craters. Shows it correlates NETWORK faults, not just GPU.
